M113 G4 DK LMT

This content was translated from Danish using AI.

The vehicle in front of you is an armoured personnel carrier (APC) of the M113 G4 DK type. It was originally an M113 A1, brought into service around 1969 and rebuilt as a G4 in 2011-2012. The vehicle served in Afghanistan and was most recently operational with the Danish Artillery Regiment as a light mortar APC.

The vehicle is maintained by volunteers here at the museum. It is still drivable and occasionally takes part in the museum's driving events, such as activity days.

About the M113 G4 DK

The G4 concept is a further development of the M113 G3, Long1. Compared with the G3 version, the G4 has, for example, a reinforced floor that provides better protection against mines and roadside bombs, which were a challenge during the war in Afghanistan.

Top-mounted seats have also been installed to protect the crew from the effects of explosions. In addition, it is equipped with, among other things, an automatic fire-suppression system, a luggage rack at the rear, storage boxes for equipment on the deck, a compressor and a thermal driving camera.

The reinforced armour in particular has contributed to a substantial increase in the G4's weight. Depending on its configuration, the G4 DK version therefore weighs around 18 tonnes.

The G4 is equipped with the same "powerpack" (engine and transmission) as the G3 models:

  • The engine is a 10.6-litre MTU V6 183 TC22 turbocharged diesel engine with an intercooler, producing approximately 300 hp.
  • The transmission is a ZF LGS 1000 hydraulic gearbox with six forward and two reverse gears.

Unlike the M113 A1 and A2 models, which are controlled using steering levers, the G3 and G4 models have a steering wheel and hydraulic steering.

The G4 version is the last version of the M113 family to have served in the Danish Army. It was introduced in 2011 and phased out in 2022, when it was replaced by the Piranha family.

Here at the museum, we have two M113 G4 DK APCs.
