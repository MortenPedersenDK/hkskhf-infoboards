M113 APC

This content was translated from Danish using AI.

The M113 A1 armoured personnel carrier (APC) is an American-developed tracked vehicle intended for travel off-road and on roads. The model also has limited amphibious capability. Approximately 80,000 units have been produced worldwide in different configurations, making the M113 probably the most widespread armoured vehicle ever.

The first M113 entered service with the Danish Army in 1964 and was used in many configurations, including:

  • Infantry transport vehicle
  • Mortar APC
  • Maintenance APC
  • Ambulance APC
  • Anti-tank missile APC
  • Fire-control centre (artillery)
  • Observer APC
  • Command APC
  • Armoured engineer APC
  • Mortar radar

A total of 650 vehicles were purchased.

The M113 A1 and A2 were phased out in 2005. Two hundred and fifty vehicles were rebuilt as the G3 model. The A1 and A2 were controlled using steering levers, allowing the track on the side towards which the vehicle was turning to be braked. The G3 and G4 models instead received a small steering wheel. At the same time, the fuel tanks were mounted externally, the armour was improved, the steel tracks were replaced by rubber tracks, and a more powerful engine was installed. The conversion was carried out by Flensburger Fahrzeugbau GmbH.
